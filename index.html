About

Financial Accounting & ESG Reporting Lab

FAER specializes in interdisciplinary research at the intersection of financial accounting, machine learning, and environmental, social, and governance factors.

The lab aims to explore innovative ways to incorporate ESG data into financial accounting, leading to better-informed business decisions and a more sustainable future.

We provide a collaborative and inclusive environment where members can develop rigorous research skills, ask new empirical questions, and contribute to important societal challenges.

Research

What We Study

Financial reporting, ESG, green finance, and AI methods.

01

ESG & Financial Reporting

Sustainability reporting, ESG disclosure, annual reports, and the information environment around firms.

02

Capital Market & Green Finance

Investor response, M&A, valuation, sustainable finance, and the market implications of non-financial information.

03

Carbon Accounting & Credit

Internal carbon pricing, firm performance, carbon credit markets, and measurement systems for environmental accountability.
